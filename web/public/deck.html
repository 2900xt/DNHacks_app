When a supplier goes down

A shortlist, not an optimiser.

We rank who holds an active filing and a US registration — by whether moving there actually diversifies, and whether the alternate has its own trouble.

What we cannot tell you, printed on every row: plant capacity, lead time, or whether they would sell to you. Nobody publishes those. An optimiser without a capacity constraint is a sort, not an optimisation.
